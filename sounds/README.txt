Put your own sounds here (mp3, ogg, wav, m4a).

Loops\    repeat until you switch them off (rain, tavern, wind, music).
Effects\  play once when clicked (door, sword, scream).

Any other folder you make here becomes its own group of one-shot sounds;
put "loop" in a folder or file name to make it repeat.
New files show up in the control window within 20 seconds.
